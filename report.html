Incident response

How to Get Help

If you or someone you know has been scammed, hacked, or had personal data exposed, act quickly. Follow these steps and contact the right authorities.

01

Your first 15 minutes

Complete these immediate steps to protect your accounts and preserve evidence.

  • The scammer's phone number, username, profile link, or email address.
  • The full conversation or message content.
  • Transaction receipts, reference numbers, bank account numbers, or e-wallet details used by the scammer.
  • Exact dates and timestamps.
0 of 3 done
02

Choose a reporting channel

Contact the Philippine agency best suited to your situation.

1. Cybercrime Investigation and Coordinating Center (CICC)

Best For
Immediate cybercrime assistance, online scams, phishing, and coordinated inter-agency response.
Website
https://cicc.gov.ph/report
Email
report@cicc.gov.ph
24/7 Hotline
1326 (Toll-free nationwide)
Report to CICC Online

2. Philippine National Police — Anti-Cybercrime Group (PNP-ACG)

Best For
Online fraud, identity theft, hacked social media accounts, cyber-extortion, and online harassment.
Website
https://acg.pnp.gov.ph
Email
acg@pnp.gov.ph or csradacgroup@gmail.com
Contact Number
Hotline: 87230401 | Local: 7488 | Contact No.: 09688658484
In-Person Reporting
Visit the nearest Regional Anti-Cybercrime Unit (RACU) office with printed copies of your screenshots and valid IDs.

3. National Bureau of Investigation — Cybercrime Division (NBI-CCD)

Best For
Formal criminal complaints, digital forensics, and complex cybercrime investigations.
Website
https://nbi.gov.ph
Email
ccd@nbi.gov.ph
Contact Number
(02) 8523-8231 to 38
In-Person Reporting
Submit a formal complaint affidavit and supporting digital evidence at the NBI Cybercrime Division office.

4. National Privacy Commission (NPC)

Best For
Data breaches, organizations mishandling or leaking your personal information, unauthorized disclosure of student/employee records, and harassment by abusive online lending apps.
Website
https://privacy.gov.ph
Email
complaints@privacy.gov.ph
