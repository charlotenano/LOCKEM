OUR PHILOSOPHY & ORIGIN

Digital Literacy for an Empowered Nation

LOCK-EM is a student-led cybersecurity awareness advocacy created by Computer Science students for our Reading Visual Arts course. As students building software, studying networks, and learning how systems are defended, we realized that the people who need cybersecurity advice most are often shut out by how complicated it sounds. We make practical, verified guidance easier to understand and use.

OUR GOAL

As future developers and tech professionals, we believe security should be accessible. We translate what we learn in our Computer Science classes into plain language and practical steps anyone can use to protect personal data, recognize digital red flags, and understand their rights.

Serving:Students & AcademiaE-Wallet & Banking UsersGovernment Portal UsersMSMEs

Our Core Commitments

Built upon transparent student principles that prioritize user protection above all else.

01

Integrity

Cybersecurity should not rely on panic or clickbait. We stick to verified and accurate information grounded in official advisories from Philippine agencies to help you stay cautious without feeling overwhelmed.

Evidence-Based Guidelines
02

Clarity

We take the technical concepts behind phishing, malware, and data privacy and break them down into straightforward steps that make sense even if you don't come from a tech background.

Plain-Language Translation
03

Action

Knowing about a threat is only half the battle. Every issue we cover is paired with clear, realistic steps to secure your account and direct links to the right Philippine authorities when you need real help.
