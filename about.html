About LOCK-EM

Making cybersecurity understandable, accessible, and actionable for every Filipino.

Our Mission

"Digital Literacy for an Empowered Nation"

Our Goal

LOCK-EM is a student-led cybersecurity awareness advocacy created by Computer Science students for our Reading Visual Arts course. As Computer Science students building software, studying networks, and learning how systems are defended, we realized something important. The people who need cybersecurity advice the most are often shut out by how complicated it sounds. Most security guides are packed with heavy technical jargon, leaving everyday internet users unprotected against scams and data leaks they could have easily avoided.

As future developers and tech professionals, we believe security should not be gatekept behind technical terms. Our goal is to translate what we learn in our Computer Science classes into practical and plain language anyone can use. Whether you're a fellow student using a school portal, a person managing mobile banking, or a citizen accessing government services online, we want you to feel confident spotting digital red flags, protecting your personal data, and knowing your rights.

Our Core Commitments

01

Integrity

Cybersecurity should not rely on panic or clickbait. We stick to verified and accurate information grounded in official advisories from Philippine agencies to help you stay cautious without feeling overwhelmed.

02

Clarity

We take the technical concepts behind phishing, malware, and data privacy and break them down into straightforward steps that make sense even if you don't come from a tech background.

03

Action

Knowing about a threat is only half the battle. Every issue we cover is paired with clear, realistic steps to secure your account and direct links to the right Philippine authorities when you need real help.
