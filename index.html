Why Cybersecurity Matters

The Philippines is one of the most active digital nations in the world. We rely on smartphones for banking, online classes, shopping, and accessing public services. However, rapid digital adoption has also made everyday Filipinos prime targets for online fraud, identity theft, and data leaks. Good cybersecurity starts with knowing the warning signs and practicing basic digital practices.

4 Habits to Secure Your Accounts Today

Practical steps you can take today.

01

Turn On Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Add a second layer of protection to your email, social media, and e-wallets so a stolen password alone is not enough to break in.

02

Never Click Links in Texts

Legitimate banks, couriers, and government agencies do not send clickable login links via SMS.

03

Use Unique Passwords

Never reuse the same password across your school portal, personal email, and banking apps.

04

Keep Devices Updated

Software updates patch security holes that hackers actively exploit.
