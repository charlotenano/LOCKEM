However, rapid digital adoption has also made everyday Filipinos prime targets for online fraud, identity theft, and data leaks. BASELINE DEFENSE ROUTINES

4 Habits to Secure Your Accounts Today

Essential, zero-cost security routines engineered for every Filipino smartphone user and digital transactor.

HABIT 01

Turn On Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Add a second layer of protection to your email, social media, and e-wallets so a stolen password alone is not enough to break in.

HABIT 02

Never Click Links in Texts

Legitimate banks, couriers, and government agencies do not send clickable login links via SMS. Always access through verified direct domains.

HABIT 03

Use Unique Passwords

Never reuse the same password across your school portal, personal email, and banking apps. One compromised portal compromises all.

HABIT 04

Keep Devices Updated

Software updates patch discovered security holes and zero-day vulnerabilities that hackers actively exploit across mobile systems.
